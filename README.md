# Dear-Data
My data project examines how the amount of food that is consumed throughout the day, to show the impact it can have on my energy levels. This is especially important as the levels of energy we feel during the day can significantly effect our day-to-day academic performance and overall health whether it is physical or mental.  
I will be using Processing to create a visualisation showing the data i have collected, consisting of the food i eat, the time i eat and recording the energy levels. My aim is to ensure that my data creation is presented creatively and can easy to understand. I will be using Microsoft excel to store all of my data and will be saving a copy to my folder after every change then making a commit on GitHub.
