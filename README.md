# Dear-Data
My data project examines how the amount of food that is consumed throughout the day affects my energy levels. This is especially important, as the levels of energy we feel during the day can significantly affect our day-to-day academic performance and overall health, whether it is physical or mental.  I will be using Processing to create a visualisation showing the data I have collected, consisting of the food I eat, the time I eat and the energy levels I record. My aim is to ensure that my data creation is presented creatively and is easy to understand. I will be using Microsoft Excel to store all of my data and will be saving a copy to my folder after every change, then making a commit on GitHub. Almost every day I make meaningful commits, as this is a way to earn marks. With additional marks for maintaining a commit span of over one week and over two weeks. Up until the 4th of November, I would need to collect at least 25 data items, with each item potentially earning 1 mark.
